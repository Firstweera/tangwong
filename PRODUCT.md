# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TangWong is for groups of friends who want to play social board games together on phones, usually during casual hangouts, remote calls, or quick online sessions. Users need to create rooms fast, invite friends with minimal friction, understand who is ready, chat, bluff, vote, and play without leaking hidden information.

## Product Purpose

TangWong exists to make online board game nights feel as easy as opening a voice room with friends. The product succeeds when a group can move from Home to Create/Join Room to Lobby to a complete Avalon-inspired match on mobile, with clear state, trustworthy privacy, and enough social energy to feel like everyone is actually around the same table.

## Brand Personality

Social, playful, premium. The interface should feel modern and lively like a hangout app, grounded enough for serious hidden-information games, and polished enough that users trust it with multiplayer state.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, beige/cream board-game nostalgia, heavy decorative glassmorphism, copied real board game branding, desktop-first lobbies, and visual clutter that makes turn/vote/ready state hard to parse.

## Design Principles

1. The room is the product. Every screen should make it obvious how to gather friends, see status, and continue the match.
2. Mobile is the default table. Controls must be thumb-friendly, dense enough for play, and readable in low-light social settings.
3. Hidden information earns trust. Private state, role reveals, and action affordances should be visually clear and never ambiguous.
4. Playful, not childish. Mascots, chips, cards, and motion add warmth, while spacing, contrast, and hierarchy keep the app premium.
5. Preserve the prototype's tabletop identity while migrating toward reusable components and tokens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for primary text and controls. Support reduced motion, avoid color-only state indicators, keep touch targets at least 44px where practical, and preserve readable Thai/English mixed-language typography.
